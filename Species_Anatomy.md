# SS13 Species & Anatomy Quick-Guide
*(one-liners for the most common playable races)*

- **Human** — default pink-to-brown skin, no extras.
- **Skrell** — smooth amphibian skin (blue-green-purple range), head crests / frills, no visible ears.
- **Tajaran** — fur-covered feline; muzzle, tail, digitigrade legs, slit pupils.
- **Vox** — avian-raptor; elongated beak, feathers on head/back, tall digitigrade legs, feathery tail.
- **Unathi** — reptilian; scales (green-brown), swept-back cranial fins/horns, thick tail.
- **IPC** — sleek metal/plastic synthetic; glowing faceplate, limb joint seams, no flesh tones.
- **Diona** — plant-humanoid; bark plating, leaf or vine hair, glowing green eyes.
- **Plasmaman** — glowing skeletal frame inside pressurized purple suit/helmet.
- **Slime Person** — semi-translucent gelatin body (bright color), slime “hair,” droplet hands.

> Always trust sprite palette over lore if the two conflict.  
> If a trait isn’t visible in the sprite, ask for confirmation before adding it.
